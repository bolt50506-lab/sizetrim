DEPLOY (free, ~5 minutes)
1. Buy/choose a domain. Edit DOMAIN, SITE (brand) and contact email at the top of build.py and in contact/privacy text, then run: python3 build.py
2. Upload this whole folder to Netlify (drag-and-drop at app.netlify.com/drop) or Cloudflare Pages. Connect your domain.
3. Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
4. After traffic starts, apply for Google AdSense (needs About, Privacy, Contact pages: already included). When approved, put your ca-pub ID and slot ID in build.py and rebuild.
