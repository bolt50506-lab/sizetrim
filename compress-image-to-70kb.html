Compress Image to 70KB

Need your photo under 70KB for an upload form? Choose your image below and the tool shrinks it to just under 70KB while keeping it as sharp as the limit allows.

  • Free
  • 100% private
  • No sign-up

How to use

  1. Select your image (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Keep 70KB selected as the target, or type your own size.
  3. Press Compress, check the preview, then download the result.

About 70KB

70KB is a middle size used by some university and recruitment forms. Skin tones and printed text on badges usually stay readable.

For scale, 70KB is about 0.07MB. A typical 3MB phone photo has to shrink roughly 44 times to fit, so expect lower resolution and some softness. Cropping first keeps more detail.

Tips for best results

Portals that ask for 70KB usually also want a JPG file. This tool outputs JPG. If the result looks too soft, upload a higher-resolution original, because tiny or already-compressed images have little left to give.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens inside your browser and your file never leaves your device.

Why is my result slightly under the target?

The tool finds the highest quality that still fits under the limit you chose.
